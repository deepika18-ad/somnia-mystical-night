    THE SCIENCE BENEATH THE STARS

    Curiosity, guided
    by responsible AI.

    Somnia is a capstone web application demonstrating how a trained machine-learning pipeline can classify patterns in tabular sleep and lifestyle data.

    ⌘

    Machine learning

    A scikit-learn preprocessing and Random Forest pipeline is trained from your verified dataset. Results must be evaluated on held-out data.

    ▤

    Account-based history

    Flask handles sessions and SQLite stores account and assessment records. Backend queries restrict history to the signed-in owner.

    ♡

    Responsible boundaries

    Sleep-hygiene suggestions are general information. Somnia does not prescribe treatment or confirm a health condition.
